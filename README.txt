Off the Rail — photo catalog demo

Open index.html in a browser. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.
Alternatively, open off-the-rail-standalone.html (no image files needed).

30 generated product images: 10 original front views, 10 original side views and 10 alternate front colourways.
Only the original-colour side view was generated; on the clothing rail the side view stays the original colour when the alternate swatch is selected, while the front view and detail image show the selected colour.
The shopping bag, stock counts and notifications are demo-only. No backend is connected.
